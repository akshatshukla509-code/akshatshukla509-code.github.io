About

I work where hardware, software and storytelling meet. On the engineering side I build survey tools: a ground rover and a drone that capture land data. On the web side I design and ship websites. On the creative side I edit video, and I publish that work on Instagram.

Being able to build the system, write the code and then make a clear video about it is useful on a team. It means fewer hand-offs and a demo that shows what the work actually does.

Projects

Land survey rover

Completed

Ground robot for measuring terrain

A wheeled rover that moves across a site and records position data, so land can be measured faster and with less manual effort than a traditional walk-through.

  • Designed the drive and control system for steady movement over uneven ground.
  • Wrote the firmware and software that read positioning sensors and log survey points.
  • Tested in the field and tuned the rover from the results.
  • Embedded code
  • GNSS positioning
  • Motor control
  • Data logging

Survey drone

Completed

Aerial capture for mapping land

A drone built to cover survey areas from the air. It complements the rover by capturing large areas quickly and giving a view of the whole site.

  • Built and configured the airframe, flight controller and sensors.
  • Planned flight paths so the drone covers an area with consistent overlap.
  • Worked on turning captured data into usable survey output.
  • Flight controller
  • Mission planning
  • Aerial imaging
  • Python
